Our Mission

WE PRESENT GOD TO KIDS
IN A FUN & CREATIVE WAY

God's Heritage is the children's ministry of RCCG Mercy Court — a safe, vibrant space where kids from 6 weeks to 12 years encounter God, make friends, and grow in faith through engaging worship, Bible teaching, and hands-on activities.

Age Groups

Who We Serve

6 weeks – 5 years

Early Childhood

Each week your little ones experience age-appropriate Bible stories, worship, and activities that help them begin their relationship with God. Every child can be secure knowing that God made them, God loves them, and Jesus wants to be their friend forever.

6 – 12 years

Elementary

Kids connect with God and each other through interactive worship, engaging Bible lessons, and small group activities. We help them discover who God is, how to strengthen their faith, and how to live it out every single day.

What to Expect

Your child's safety is our top priority. Here's exactly how drop-off and pick-up works.

1

Check In

Stop by our check-in area in the lobby. Your child gets a name tag and you get a matching security label.

2

Have Fun!

Kids enjoy age-appropriate worship, Bible teaching, crafts, and activities — while parents enjoy the service.

3

Check Out

After service, show your matching security label — your child is released only to you.

Answers to Your Questions

What age does my child need to be?

God's Heritage is available for children from 6 weeks old through 12 years. We have age-appropriate environments for every stage.

Can siblings of different ages stay together?

For the best experience, children are grouped by age. Our team can accommodate special circumstances — just let us know on arrival.

Is it really safe?

Absolutely. Every volunteer is vetted, every child is checked in, and only a parent or guardian with the matching security label can pick up a child.

How can I volunteer?

We love people who love kids! Serve as a small group leader, greeter, worship helper, or behind the scenes. Contact us to get started.
