What to Expect

Your child's safety is our top priority. Here's exactly how drop-off and pick-up works.

1

Check In

Stop by our check-in area in the lobby. Your child gets a name tag and you get a matching security label.

2

Kids enjoy age-appropriate worship, Bible teaching, crafts, and activities — while parents enjoy the service.

3

Check Out

After service, show your matching security label — your child is released only to you.

Content & Resources

KIDS ARE
GROWING EVERY WEEK

God's Heritage, with the aid of expert consultants, has developed a comprehensive curriculum for our children. The curriculum is scripture-based and includes foundational Christian teachings as well as specific teachings related to the vision and mission of Mercy Court and God's Heritage.

We implement our curriculum for all ages, as appropriate. The content is applicable to every child registered in our children's ministry.
