At Mercy Court, we believe that engaging with the Word of God should be more than a ritual. It should be an experience of discovery. That conviction is what led to the creation of **be.lievi.ng** — Mercy Court's first technology product and a free Bible study app built not just for the Mercy Court family, but for the world.

Introduced to the Mercy Court congregation by Dr. Sunday Ikpe and Bro. Femi Akinmade, be.lievi.ng represents a bold step at the intersection of faith and technology — one the team believes is long overdue.

<blockquote class="border-l-4 border-brand-red pl-6 my-8">
<p class="text-gray-700 text-xl italic leading-relaxed">"We are at a time when the world is rapidly changing, and we have decided to take advantage of the moment."</p>
<cite class="block mt-3 text-sm font-bold text-brand-red uppercase tracking-wide not-italic">— Dr. Sunday Ikpe</cite>
</blockquote>

## Study First. Always.

There is no shortage of Bible apps. What has been missing is one built specifically to help you go deeper — an app that makes studying the Word as intuitive and rewarding as reading it. be.lievi.ng fills that gap.

<blockquote class="border-l-4 border-brand-red pl-6 my-8">
<p class="text-gray-700 text-xl italic leading-relaxed">"We have a lot of Bible applications out there, but this is study first. The focus is on the Word, on study."</p>
<cite class="block mt-3 text-sm font-bold text-brand-red uppercase tracking-wide not-italic">— Dr. Sunday Ikpe</cite>
</blockquote>

You do not even need to sign up to begin reading. But once you register — completely free — a full suite of study features opens up. And the vision from the beginning has been broader than any single congregation.

<blockquote class="border-l-4 border-brand-red pl-6 my-8">
<p class="text-gray-700 text-xl italic leading-relaxed">"This is not just for me. This is Mercy to the world."</p>
<cite class="block mt-3 text-sm font-bold text-brand-red uppercase tracking-wide not-italic">— Dr. Sunday Ikpe</cite>
</blockquote>

## Meet Scribe: Your Personal Bible Study Assistant

At the heart of be.lievi.ng is **Scribe**, an AI-powered Bible study assistant that works alongside you as you study, prepare sermon notes, or dig into the meaning of a passage. Ask Scribe about the Greek or Hebrew meaning of a word, explore the Strong's Concordance, or request context behind a verse — all without leaving the app.

<blockquote class="border-l-4 border-brand-red pl-6 my-8">
<p class="text-gray-700 text-xl italic leading-relaxed">"You don't have to navigate to Google while you're preparing your study notes or sermon notes. What does the Greek word say? What does it mean? Everything can be done with the assistant at once."</p>
<cite class="block mt-3 text-sm font-bold text-brand-red uppercase tracking-wide not-italic">— Bro. Femi Akinmade</cite>
</blockquote>

Scribe is also built with families in mind. If you ask it anything outside the Bible, it will redirect you. "This is a safe assistant to use," Bro. Akinmade assured the congregation.

For those whose notes need a little polish, Scribe can help with that too. Ask it to rewrite a note and it will correct spelling, punctuation, and redundancies without altering the meaning — and the original always stays untouched. For teachers and preachers, this transforms rough, in-the-moment notes into something ready to share.

## Powerful Tools for Deeper Study

be.lievi.ng is built around the conviction that the tools you use shape the depth of your engagement with Scripture. Every feature has been designed with that in mind.

<div class="space-y-6">
<div class="border-l-2 border-brand-red pl-5">
<h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 text-lg mb-1">Bible Versions</h3>
<p class="text-gray-600 leading-relaxed">The app currently offers the King James Version, Bible in Basic English (BBE), and the American Standard Version (ASV). More versions are actively being pursued, with Mercy Court working directly with rights holders to expand the library.</p>
</div>
<div class="border-l-2 border-brand-red pl-5">
<h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 text-lg mb-1">Character Search</h3>
<p class="text-gray-600 leading-relaxed">A searchable index of every person mentioned in the Bible, linked directly to the verses where they appear. Click a verse and you are taken straight to it.</p>
</div>
<div class="border-l-2 border-brand-red pl-5">
<h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 text-lg mb-1">Colour-Coded Highlights</h3>
<p class="text-gray-600 leading-relaxed">Highlighting in be.lievi.ng goes beyond cosmetic. You decide what each colour represents — faith, love, hope, whatever it is. Every highlighted verse is saved and searchable, building a personal, organised reference system over time.</p>
</div>
<div class="border-l-2 border-brand-red pl-5">
<h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 text-lg mb-1">Smart Notes with Cross-Reference</h3>
<p class="text-gray-600 leading-relaxed">Type your study notes using standard Bible notation — for example, John 3:16 — and the app automatically converts it into a clickable cross-reference. Notes save automatically and can be shared with the community so others can study alongside you.</p>
</div>
<div class="border-l-2 border-brand-red pl-5">
<h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 text-lg mb-1">One-Tap Scripture Sharing</h3>
<p class="text-gray-600 leading-relaxed">Long-press any verse to generate a beautifully formatted, ready-to-share image of that scripture, complete with the verse reference. Post it directly to social media without switching apps or reformatting anything.</p>
</div>
</div>
