The New MC

Raising Pacesetters, Enjoying Life!

A vibrant youth community where you can embark on a journey of spiritual growth and make a lasting impact on the world.

Get Connected Learn More
Faith-Driven Real Community Purpose-Led

More Than Just a Church. A Family.

We are a love-bound community open to all — particularly college students — through interactive, captivating, and life-changing services. At The New MC, you'll find a place to belong, grow, and make an impact.

Our Mission

To raise up Christlike youthful pacesetters who discover, develop, and deploy their God-given potentials.

Our Vision

To model excellent living and impact our world in service to God and our dynamic generation.

"Your miracle is here. You are special."

What to Expect

Everything you need to know before your first visit.

Engaging Services

60–75 minute services held at 12:30 PM on 2nd–5th Sundays. Joint service on the first Sunday at 10 AM.

Joint Service

The full Mercy Court community comes together to worship as one. A powerful experience of unity and fellowship.

Contemporary Worship

Our choir team, the Tribe of Judah (TOJ), leads dynamic praise and worship every service.

Interactive & Dynamic

A participation-focused environment where your voice matters and engagement is encouraged.

Come As You Are

Casual dress is encouraged. Come comfortable, come as yourself — you belong here.

All-Round Excellence

Our services emphasize discipline and punctuality — because excellence honors God.

"Either you lift your hands in praise, clap, sing along, or worship quietly with reflection — you'll feel God's presence."

Find Your Place

Our serve teams are unique notes that contribute to the symphony of this ministry. There's a place for you.

The Host

Ushers and welcomers ensuring every person feels at home from the moment they walk in.

Tribe of Judah (TOJ)

Vocalists and instrumentalists leading the church in powerful praise and worship every service.

The New Media

Multimedia, design, filming, and editing specialists bringing the ministry's story to life.

The New Intercessors

Prayer warriors and intercessors standing in the gap for the ministry and community.

The First Touch

Membership care and newcomer follow-up — making sure no one feels like a stranger.

The New Connectors

Transportation coordination team ensuring everyone can get to and from services.

Leadership

Led by Pastor Tise & Toni Oyetunde

Our leaders are passionate about empowering young people to seize opportunities, make wise choices, and embrace God's purpose for their lives. Under their guidance, The New MC has become a thriving community of purpose-driven young adults.
