Our Heart

MUSIC THAT MOVES
HEAVEN AND EARTH

Tehillah Voices is the worship ministry of RCCG Mercy Court — a community of singers, musicians, and creatives who believe that authentic worship is the most powerful force on earth. The name Tehillah is a Hebrew word for praise — the kind that flows from a transformed heart. We exist to usher God's people into His presence every Sunday and through every song we release.

Who We Are

WORSHIP AS
A WAY OF LIFE

We are not just a choir or a band — we are worshippers first. Every member of Tehillah Voices is committed to a lifestyle of prayer, devotion, and excellence in craft, because we believe you can only lead others where you have been yourself.

From Sunday morning services to special events and original recordings, Tehillah Voices carries the sound of RCCG Mercy Court — bold, Spirit-filled, and rooted in the Word.
