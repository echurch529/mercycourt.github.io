Who We Are

WE ARE
PACESETTERS

We are dedicated to raising Christ-like pacesetters who through God's mercy maximize their potentials and enjoy life. Our mission centers on fostering a sense of community where faith has the power to transform lives.

At Mercy Court, we believe that everyone has unique gifts and potential. We're here to help you discover, develop, and deploy those gifts for Kingdom impact while experiencing the fullness of life God intended.
