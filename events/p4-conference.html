Samson Ajetomobi is a pastor, teacher, author and conference speaker with an apostolic and prophetic mandate for revival and leadership development. He is the Founder and President of the Men of Issachar Vision (MIV), based in Ibadan, Nigeria, a ministry committed to raising discerning believers who understand the times and provide godly leadership in every sphere of life. Under his leadership, MIV has grown into a movement spanning local assemblies, leadership and missions training, prayer retreats and global conferences for ministers and marketplace leaders. Known for his uncompromising emphasis on holiness, prayer and spiritual maturity, Rev. Ajetomobi mentors ministers across denominational lines and has helped birth church leaders, intercessory networks and mission initiatives throughout Africa and beyond. In 2023, he was appointed Chairman of the Pentecostal Fellowship of Nigeria (PFN), Oyo State Chapter.
